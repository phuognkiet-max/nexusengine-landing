# Privacy notice — copy for the static website

Last updated: October 8, 2026

NexusEngine’s website introduces an early-stage video localization project and provides an email contact link.

## Information on this website

This version of the website does not include a registration form, account system, video upload feature, or analytics tracking added by NexusEngine. The illustrative workflow does not send video or transcript content to an AI service.

The website’s hosting provider may process standard technical access information, such as an IP address, request time, browser information, and the requested page, as part of serving and securing the website. Hosting practices are governed by the provider’s own policies.

## Contacting us

Selecting an email link opens your email application. If you send a message to founder@nexusengine.id.vn, the message may include your email address, name, message content, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your inquiry. Email transmission and storage also involve your email provider and the email service used by NexusEngine.

Please do not email passwords, API keys, or confidential video files as part of an initial inquiry.

## Contact and changes

For questions about this website or an email you have sent us, contact founder@nexusengine.id.vn.

This notice should be updated before adding features that collect additional information, such as sign-up forms, video uploads, user accounts, or analytics.
