A FEW DETAILS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Can I upload and process videos here?+

Not yet. This is the NexusEngine product website. Processing currently runs in our Windows desktop prototype. Contact the founder to discuss early access or a product demonstration.

Does the prototype already use Claude?+

No. The current prototype supports Gemini and DeepSeek for translation. We plan to add Claude for contextual transcreation of product messaging and calls to action.

What does the desktop prototype support?+

Speech recognition, OCR for on-screen text, subtitle editing and translation, local synthetic voiceovers, caption styling, and video export. Available languages and voices depend on the selected models and installed voice library. Output requires human review.

How can I see the product or request access?+

Email founder@nexusengine.id.vn with your target language and the type of product videos you work with. The sample explorer on this website illustrates the workflow using prewritten captions. It is not a live processing demo; contact the founder to see the actual desktop prototype.
